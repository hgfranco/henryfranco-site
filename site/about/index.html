A little about me

Hi, I'm Henry.

An engineering manager with a curiosity for how things work—and a soundtrack for most of it.

I enjoy working through a problem, understanding its pieces, and making something useful out of what I learn. This site is a place to share that process.

People & systems

Engineering, with curiosity.

I work in engineering management, and I like staying close to the technical work. My personal projects give me room to explore the details: a Linux server, a networking problem, or an automation that makes the next step easier.

Recently, that has meant building a Kubernetes cluster on AWS, managing infrastructure with Terraform, and configuring it with Ansible. I'm also studying for the Certified Kubernetes Administrator exam, learning by running the commands and seeing what happens.

Learning by building.

What Is Henry Listening To? brings two interests together: music and code. It shares my Spotify listening, and gives me a practical reason to keep learning about deployments, storage, networking, and running an application.

This site runs on the same cluster. You can explore the projects and infrastructure, or look through my work on GitHub.

Beyond the terminal

Usually listening to something.

Music and podcasts are part of my day. Sometimes they lead to another idea to build; sometimes they're just a good companion while I figure something out.

I'm keeping this corner of the internet personal: things I've made, things I'm learning, and a little of what I enjoy along the way.
