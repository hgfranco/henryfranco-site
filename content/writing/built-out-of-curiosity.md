This site is where I share my technical projects and what I'm learning. Writing is the next part of that: a place to slow down and explain the things I'm trying to understand.

## Following the questions

Lately, those questions have been about AWS, Kubernetes, Terraform, and Ansible. How do the pieces fit together? What happens between a request arriving and an application answering it? What does a managed service take care of?

The goal here is to follow those questions through small projects, diagrams, and notes.

## What to expect

Notes on Kubernetes and infrastructure. Thoughts on engineering leadership. The occasional detour into music or something else that catches my attention.

> Here's something I wanted to understand, here's how I approached it, here's what confused me, and here's what finally made it click.

## What I want to learn

Writing an explanation should help uncover the parts I still need to understand. These notes will be a record of that process.
